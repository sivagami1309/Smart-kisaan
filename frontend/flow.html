Login never blocks prices

A phone number is asked for only for future price alerts (PRD: no login to view prices).

Every price is dated

Yesterday’s price is flagged; older prices are listed as “no report today”, never ranked and never ₹0.

Costs flow everywhere

Transport and commission set in “Me” or on the results screen change the ranking, the offer check and the SMS reply.
